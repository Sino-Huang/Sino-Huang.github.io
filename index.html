One-Line Bio

Born in 1997. PhD from the University of Melbourne. My research focuses on large language models and neuro-symbolic systems for autonomous robot decision-making. I have led/participated in multiple major international research grants, published 7 papers at top-tier venues including AAAI, ICAPS, ACL, and ICLR, and serve as a reviewer for ICAPS and ACL.
